About the publication

Pelagic Research covers listed companies in marine industries, energy and sustainability. Companies in these sectors tend to need a lot of capital, their earnings move with the cycle, and regulation affects them heavily. That makes them hard to value, and the market sometimes gets the price wrong.

One or two reports come out each month, and each one covers a single company. Every report follows the same structure, so they are easy to compare.

Business and industry

How the company makes money, how it compares with competitors, and the state of supply and demand in its industry.

Thesis

Why the share price looks wrong, and what could cause it to change.

Valuation

A financial model with bull, base and bear cases, and a 12-month price target.

Risks

The main things that could make the thesis wrong.

Ratings

Each rating is based on the expected return over the next 12 months, measured from the reference share price and date noted in the report.

RatingExpected 12-month returnWhat it means
BuyAbove +20%I expect the stock to rise substantially. The share price looks well below what the business is worth.
Accumulate+10% to +20%I expect a solid gain, with less upside than a Buy. The stock looks undervalued, but by a smaller margin.
Hold−10% to +10%I see the stock as roughly fairly valued. The price could move either way, with no strong case in one direction.
SellBelow −10%I expect the stock to fall significantly. The share price looks higher than the business is worth.

Industry or thematic reports that don't recommend a specific stock are marked Not Rated.
