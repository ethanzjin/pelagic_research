Process

How each report comes together

Screen

Hunt for mispriced names across our three sectors — overlooked, misunderstood, or at an inflection point.

Dive

Primary filings, industry data, fleet and asset-level analysis, and conversations with people in the industry.

Model

Build an independent operating and valuation model with explicit bull, base and bear scenarios.

Publish

A clear thesis, a price target, the key risks, and a full PDF — released once a month.
