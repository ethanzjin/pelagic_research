Write with us

Pelagic Research is opening up to a few more people. If there's a company in marine, energy or sustainability you'd like to dig into, we'd love to hear about it.

What you'd be working on

Each report covers one listed company and ends with a rating and a 12-month price target. It follows the same general shape as the other reports on the site, with sections chosen to fit the company. The methodology explains each section and how the ratings work.

You'd work with Ethan, who started Pelagic Research, on the research, the model and the write-up. He edits each report before it goes up, and everyone who worked on it is credited.

How it works

  1. I

    Pitch a company

    Send a few sentences on what the company does and why you think the share price is wrong.

  2. II

    Talk it through

    We read every pitch and agree on the angle together before any work starts.

  3. III

    Research and model

    You take on the filings, the industry data and parts of the model and draft, with regular check-ins along the way.

  4. IV

    Publish

    After a final edit and a check on the rating and price target, the report goes up on the site with you credited on it.
