# UX profile — Pelagic Research

Read by the VectorLab UX skills before layout, colour, type or motion changes.

## Aesthetic
Apple native: quiet, type-led, generous whitespace. Deep-navy hero and footer; white and
#F5F5F7 content surfaces. One accent hue: brand blue #4248ED (links #2F35D6).

## Touch / focus
WCAG 2.1 AA. Hit targets 44×44px (`--hit`). Visible 2px brand focus ring.

## Type
System font (SF Pro on Apple devices). Minor Third scale from a 1rem body
(`--step-1` … `--step-7`); secondary 0.875rem, meta 0.75rem. Weights 400 / 500 / 600.
Exception: the homepage wordmark ("Pelagic", `.hero-word`) is display imagery, sized to the
viewport (up to 20rem) rather than a scale step.

## Spacing
8pt grid via `--space-*` tokens; 4pt for micro gaps. Prose 70ch.

## Breakpoints
narrow `max-width: 767.98px`; medium and up above that; prove at 375px and 1280px.

## Banned
Rainbow or multi-hue gradients on buttons; cartoon people; pills around static metadata;
blur without a visible edge and an opaque fallback.

## Motion
Timed transitions use 100 / 150 / 200 / 300ms only, ease-out to enter and ease-in to exit,
on transform and opacity (colour ≤150ms). No overshoot on interface chrome.

Notes (exceptions, from the apple-design skill):
- Gesture-driven motion (the report sheet, the segmented control's thumb, the hero manta)
  uses critically damped springs in `assets/js/motion.js`. These follow the finger and
  inherit its velocity, so they have no fixed duration. Chrome springs never overshoot.
- The hero manta is imagery, not chrome: when thrown it may settle with a small bounce.
- Homepage scroll effects are tied 1:1 to the scroll position, not timed.
