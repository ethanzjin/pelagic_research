# UX profile — Pelagic Research

Read by the VectorLab UX skills before layout, colour, type or motion changes.

## Aesthetic
The Pelagic Log: a researcher's field log from the open sea, set like a printed journal.
- Paper and ink: grained paper (#F4F1E8), blue graph-paper sections (#EBE6D8), raised cards
  (#FBF9F3), ink #16181D. Deep-navy bands and footer are "the deep". One accent hue, brand blue
  #4248ED (links and pen annotations #2F35D6); a muted red rule marks index cards.
- Masthead header on paper with a 3px double rule. Inner pages open on a paper cover (title +
  double rule), not a photograph.
- Photographs are mounted as figure plates (1px ink border, a second card peeking behind, an
  italic "Fig. n" caption) and sepia-toned until hovered. The contact band stays full-bleed.
- Homepage sections are log entries: a sticky margin column (≥900px) gives the ocean depth zone,
  the depth in serif italics and a blue-pen note (Epipelagic 100 m → Hadopelagic 6,000 m); a
  depth gauge on the right edge (≥1360px) descends with scroll.
- The latest report is an index card (ruled lines, red rule, rating as a rubber stamp); earlier
  reports are a ruled ledger; reports carry issue numbers (No. 01, No. 02, …).
- Corners 2px; primary buttons carry a hard 3px ink offset shadow, not soft elevation.
The layer lives in `assets/css/journal.css` (after `style.css`); homepage-only motion in
`assets/js/ambient.js` and `assets/js/depth.js`.

## Touch / focus
WCAG 2.1 AA. Hit targets 44×44px (`--hit`). Visible 2px brand focus ring.

## Type
Two families: Newsreader (Google Fonts, serif) for headings, the wordmark, stat figures and
journal numerals; the system sans (SF Pro on Apple devices) for body and UI. Figures use lining,
tabular numerals. Running heads and labels: 0.75rem uppercase, 0.1em tracking, weight 600.
Minor Third scale from a 1rem body (`--step-1` … `--step-7`); secondary 0.875rem, meta 0.75rem.
Weights 400 / 500 / 600 (serif headings at 500). The homepage hero headline may run past the
scale (up to 4.5rem) over its photograph.

## Spacing
8pt grid via `--space-*` tokens; 4pt for micro gaps. Prose 70ch.

## Breakpoints
narrow `max-width: 767.98px`; medium and up above that; prove at 375px and 1280px.

## Banned
Rainbow or multi-hue gradients on buttons; cartoon people; pills around static metadata;
blur without a visible edge and an opaque fallback.

## Motion
Timed transitions use 100 / 150 / 200 / 300ms only, ease-out to enter and ease-in to exit,
on transform and opacity (colour ≤150ms). No overshoot on interface chrome.

Notes (exceptions, from the apple-design skill):
- Gesture-driven motion (the report sheet, the segmented control's thumb) uses critically
  damped springs in `assets/js/motion.js`. These follow the finger and inherit its velocity,
  so they have no fixed duration. They never overshoot.
- Homepage scroll effects are tied 1:1 to the scroll position, not timed.

Delight budget (rare, once-per-visit moments; marketing durations allowed; all fade-only or off
under reduced motion):
- Cover entrance: headline words rise (700ms, 45ms stagger), the hero plate develops upward with
  clip-path (1100ms), the cover's double rule draws in (900ms).
- Section arrival: each margin rule draws in (700ms); the rating stamp lands (scale 1.35 → 1,
  200ms) and the scenario bar grows from today's price (500ms) when the index card is revealed.
- Ambient: marine snow drifts in the deep band (canvas, only while on screen); a bioluminescent
  glow trails the pointer over the contact band (underdamped spring, mouse/trackpad only).
